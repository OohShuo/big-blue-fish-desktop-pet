# Merged idle/event playback

After each normal animation, draw from the union of Idle and Event. Remove only
the independent activity interval. Set ordinary idle/event base weights to 100.
Preserve every special action rule and its metadata: night, season, festival,
meal, morning and movement weights/conditions; click/drag/greetings/farewell and
daily brushing keep their original triggers and rules. Keep meal settings.
The four disabled entries remain unchanged. Low-power playback pauses before
the next draw; a released drag must queue playback after its segment completes.
